About kcal Calculator

kcal Calculator is a free tool that estimates how many calories you burn each day and how much to eat to lose, maintain, or gain weight. It also splits your target into protein, carbs, and fat, turns those into everyday foods, and shows a rough timeline if you enter a goal weight.

How it works

Everything is calculated in your browser using standard equations (Mifflin-St Jeor and Katch-McArdle). Nothing you type is sent to us or stored. The guide shows the formulas with a worked example.

Who it is for

Adults aged 18 and over who want a clear starting point. It is not a substitute for advice from a doctor or dietitian.
