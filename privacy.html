Privacy Policy

Last updated: Nov 21st 2026

1. What the calculator does with your information

The kcal Calculator works in your browser. The age, sex, height, weight, activity level, and any other details you enter are used only to calculate your results on your screen. We do not send, store, or sell these details, and we do not create an account for you.

2. Cookies, ads, and analytics

This site may show ads from Google AdSense and may use analytics tools to understand how the site is used. These services may set cookies or collect information such as pages visited, device type, approximate location, and ad interactions.

  • Google uses cookies to show ads based on your previous visits to this site and other sites.
  • You can manage personalised ads at Google Ads Settings.
  • You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings. Some parts of the site may work less well without them.

3. Third-party links

The site may link to other websites, such as product or service pages. We are not responsible for their privacy practices, so please read their policies before sharing any information with them.

4. Children

This site is not designed for children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information through a third-party service, contact us and we will help remove it where possible.

5. Health information

Calorie and macro results are general estimates and are not medical advice. Talk to a doctor or dietitian before changing your diet, especially if you are pregnant, have a medical condition, or take medication that affects weight or appetite.

6. Your choices

You can use the calculator without giving us any personal details beyond what you type in, and you can control cookies and ad personalisation as described above.

7. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top shows when it was last changed.
